OUR PROCESS

Professional cleaning from start to finish

At VSH Tank Cleaning, every tank is cleaned through a structured process. Our team focuses on removing visible dirt as well as accumulated sludge and deposits from the tank interior.

01
🔍

Tank Inspection

We begin by inspecting the tank and checking its overall condition, size, access point and amount of accumulated dirt or sludge.

First step: Understand the tank condition before cleaning.
02
💧

Water Removal

Remaining water is removed from the tank so that the interior surface can be properly accessed and cleaned.

Goal: Prepare the tank for deep cleaning.
03
🧹

Sludge & Dirt Removal

Accumulated sludge, mud, dirt and deposits are removed from the bottom and other accessible areas of the tank.

Deep cleaning: Heavy deposits are removed before washing.
04
🧽

Manual Scrubbing

Tank walls and surfaces are manually scrubbed to loosen dirt, stains and deposits that remain attached to the tank surface.

Detail work: Surfaces are thoroughly scrubbed.
05
🚿

Pressure Washing

The tank is washed to remove loosened dirt and remaining residue from the interior surfaces.

Result: Cleaner tank surfaces and removal of remaining residue.
06
✨

Final Inspection

After cleaning, the tank is checked to ensure that the cleaning process has been completed properly before the service is finished.

Final check: Cleanliness is reviewed before completion.
