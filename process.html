Our Tank Cleaning Process

A thorough cleaning process designed to remove dirt, sludge and deposits from your water tank.

01. Inspection

We inspect the tank before starting.

  • Check tank size
  • Inspect sludge level
  • Check access points
  • Plan cleaning

02. Drain & Remove

The tank is prepared for cleaning.

  • Drain remaining water
  • Remove sludge
  • Remove dirt and deposits
  • Prepare tank surfaces

03. Deep Cleaning

The tank is thoroughly cleaned.

  • Manual scrubbing
  • Pressure washing where suitable
  • Clean walls and floor
  • Final rinse
