✦ Our method

Clean properly. Don’t just rinse.

VSH’s cleaning flow is designed around removing the dirt first, then scrubbing and disinfecting the exposed surfaces.

01

Drain the tank

Remaining water is pumped out so the floor and walls are exposed.

02

Remove sludge

Settled mud, silt and slime are removed before scrubbing.

03

Pressure-wash & scrub

Walls, floor, corners, lid and inlet areas are cleaned thoroughly.

04

Disinfect

Cleaned surfaces are treated and rinsed before refilling.

05

Photo proof & handover

Before-and-after photos can be shared on WhatsApp and the tank is closed ready to refill.

How long does it take?

Typical household tanks can often be completed within a practical service window depending on tank size, sludge level and access. Larger underground tanks and multi-tank bookings take longer.
