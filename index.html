VSH TANK CLEANING

Water tank cleaning for homes, societies and businesses across Delhi NCR

VSH Tank Cleaning cleans the water tanks that households depend on — rooftop tanks, underground sumps and large storage tanks serving apartment blocks, offices and shops. We provide professional tank cleaning services across Delhi NCR.

Water reaches most homes after a long journey — from the municipal line or a borewell, sometimes via a tanker, into a sump and then up to a rooftop tank. Each stop can leave something behind. Fine silt settles on the floor, slime forms on the walls and algae can grow where light enters.

A proper cleaning removes this build-up at the source instead of leaving your RO, geyser and pipes to deal with it.

OUR PROCESS

What VSH Tank Cleaning includes

Every booking follows the same professional five-step cleaning method.

1

Drain the tank

Remaining water is pumped out so the floor and walls are exposed.

2

Remove the sludge

Settled mud, silt and slime are removed before scrubbing.

3

Pressure wash & scrub

Walls, floor, corners and inlet areas are thoroughly cleaned.

4

Disinfect

The cleaned surfaces are disinfected and rinsed properly.

5

Photo proof & handover

Before-and-after photos can be shared on WhatsApp after completion.
