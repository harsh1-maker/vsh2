Water tank cleaning for homes, societies and businesses across Delhi NCR

VSH Tank Cleaning cleans the water tanks that most households in Delhi NCR depend on — the plastic tank on the roof, the concrete sump under the driveway, and the large storage tanks that serve apartment blocks, offices and shops.

We provide professional cleaning for homes, housing societies, shops, offices, hotels and commercial properties across Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Gurgaon, Ghaziabad and Faridabad.

Water reaches most homes after a long journey: from the municipal line or a borewell, sometimes via a tanker, into a sump, then up to a rooftop tank.

Each stop leaves something behind. Fine silt settles on the floor, a slimy biofilm forms on the walls, and if any light gets in, algae can follow.

None of this is visible from the tap until the water starts to smell, look cloudy or leave residue in your buckets and filters. A proper cleaning removes that build-up at the source instead of leaving your RO, geyser and pipes to deal with it.

What VSH Tank Cleaning includes

Every booking — whether a 500-litre rooftop tank or a large underground reservoir — follows a proper cleaning method. Nothing is skipped on smaller tanks.

1

Drain the tank

Remaining water is pumped out so the floor and walls are properly exposed before cleaning begins.

2

Remove the sludge

Settled mud, silt and slime are removed instead of simply being stirred back into the water.

3

Pressure wash & scrub

Walls, floor, corners, lid and inlet areas are scrubbed and pressure-washed to loosen dirt and biofilm.

4

Disinfect

A suitable disinfectant is applied to cleaned surfaces before the tank is rinsed properly.

5

Photo proof & handover

Before-and-after photos can be shared on WhatsApp and the tank is handed back ready for use.

The difference between a proper clean and a quick rinse is mostly in the sludge-removal stage. Scrubbing a tank that still has sludge on the floor simply stirs the dirt into suspension, and it settles back as soon as you refill.

Our cleaning process is designed to remove the build-up first, then scrub and pressure-wash the surfaces before disinfection. Standard household tanks in the 500–2,000 litre range can usually be completed efficiently, while underground tanks and multi-tank bookings may take longer.
