What VSH tank cleaning includes

Every booking follows a clear five-step method.

01

Drain the tank

Remaining water is pumped out so the floor and walls are exposed.

02

Remove sludge

Settled mud, silt and slime are removed before scrubbing.

03

Pressure-wash & scrub

Walls, floor, corners and inlet areas are thoroughly cleaned.

04

Disinfect

Cleaned surfaces are treated and rinsed before refilling.

05

Photo proof

Before-and-after photos can be sent on WhatsApp.
