FAQ

Questions about VSH Tank Cleaning?

How often should a water tank be cleaned? +

There isn't one universal interval for every tank. Cleaning frequency depends on the tank type, water source, surrounding conditions, sediment accumulation and how the system is used. Routine inspection can help determine when cleaning is needed.

Do you clean overhead and underground tanks? +

Yes. VSH Tank Cleaning can arrange cleaning services for different types of residential and commercial water storage tanks. Contact us with your tank type and approximate capacity for more information.

Will you remove the sludge from the bottom? +

Yes. Removal of accumulated sediment and sludge is an important part of thorough tank cleaning where such material is present.

Do I need to empty the tank before the service? +

Usually the tank needs to be drained or emptied appropriately before internal cleaning. We can guide you on what needs to be done before our team arrives.

Can you clean tanks in apartments and societies? +

Yes. We can discuss requirements for larger residential properties, apartment buildings and shared water storage systems.

How do I book VSH Tank Cleaning? +

You can call us directly or send a WhatsApp message. Tell us your location, tank type and approximate tank size, and we'll help you with the next steps.
